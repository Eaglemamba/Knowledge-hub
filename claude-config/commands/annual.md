Start my annual review.

1. Create a new file at `coo-track-os/reviews/annual/` named: `YYYY.md`
2. Copy the template from `coo-track-os/reviews/annual/_template.md`
3. Read all quarterly reviews from this year in `coo-track-os/reviews/quarterly/`
4. Read `coo-track-os/memory.md` for accumulated insights
5. Read all goal files, `coo-track-os/frameworks/coo_readiness_assessment.md`, and `coo-track-os/frameworks/life_map.md`
6. Guide me through the full annual review framework in `coo-track-os/frameworks/annual_review.md`:
   - Part 1: Chronological walk-through, peaks, valleys, surprises
   - Part 2: Domain assessment (all 7 domains with satisfaction scores)
   - Part 3: Three-question deep dive on the entire year
   - Part 4: The ten-year letter
   - Part 5: Top 10 lessons, stop/start/continue, intent for next year
   - Part 6: COO-readiness honest assessment
7. After completing, guide me through updating:
   - `memory.md` with the year's key insights
   - `north_star.md` if direction has evolved
   - `goals/1_year.md`, `goals/3_year.md`, `goals/5_year_coo.md`
   - `frameworks/vivid_vision.md`
   - `frameworks/life_map.md`
8. Ask if any insights should go to `tracking/content_pipeline.md` for a year-end article
9. This takes a full day — pace accordingly
